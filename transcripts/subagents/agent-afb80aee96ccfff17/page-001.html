9	# Plan: Level the camera to world up during programmatic moves
10	
11	Concept: [OKR Viewer - Product Vision](../../Concepts/OKR%20Viewer%20-%20Product%20Vision.md)
12	(invariant 5: "the scene never disorients the user on its own"). Spike plan, one file.
13	
14	## Context
15	
16	`3d-force-graph` defaults to `TrackballControls`
17	(`web/node_modules/three-render-objects/dist/three-render-objects.mjs:486` and `:603-607`).
18	Trackball rotates `camera.up` freely (`web/node_modules/three/examples/jsm/controls/TrackballControls.js:510`),
19	so after the user tumbles, the camera carries a roll. Every programmatic move (`flyTo` at
20	`web/src/Graph.svelte:58-75`, `zoomToFit` at `:125`) goes through `cameraPosition()`
21	(`three-render-objects.mjs:306-340`), which tweens position and `controls.target` but never
22	touches `camera.up`. `TrackballControls.update()` then calls `object.lookAt(target)`
23	(`TrackballControls.js:381`) with the rolled up vector, so the whole flight is tilted, and the
24	roll about the view axis visibly changes as the lookAt target moves.
25	
26	The layout is `dagMode('td')` (`Graph.svelte:104`): world +Y is the tree's top, so "level" has
27	a real meaning.
28	
29	## Goal
30	
31	Every programmatic camera move ends with screen-up equal to world +Y, the roll is removed
32	smoothly over the flight, and user tumbling stays free (trackball, may roll).
33	
34	## Decisions (locked in)
35	
36	1. **Keep trackball.** Users can still roll. Only programmatic moves re-level. (Interview,
37	   2026-09-11.)
38	2. **Re-level, don't just preserve.** The move tweens `camera.up` from its current value to
39	   world +Y over the same duration as the position tween with the same `Quadratic.Out` easing,
40	   so a rolled camera un-rolls during the flight rather than snapping.
41	3. **Implemented in `Graph.svelte`, no library patch.** `graph.camera()`
42	   (`web/node_modules/3d-force-graph/dist/3d-force-graph.mjs:276`) exposes the THREE camera and
43	   `graph.controls()` (`:285`) the trackball. `camera.up` is read by trackball's `lookAt` every
44	   frame, so driving it from our own `animate()` loop (`Graph.svelte:77-99`) is enough.
45	4. **Degenerate view guarded.** If the view direction is within ~11° of world ±Y (looking
46	   straight down the tree), `lookAt` with up = +Y is undefined. Level toward world −Z instead.
47	5. **Trackball inertia off.** `staticMoving` defaults to `false` (`TrackballControls.js:147`),
48	   so a flick before a fly-to keeps rotating `camera.up` while we tween it. Set
49	   `staticMoving = true`. This also removes inertia from user tumbling; that is the tradeoff
50	   that makes programmatic moves deterministic. The alternative (zeroing the private
51	   `_lastAngle`) is version-fragile.

function levelCamera(cam, controls, ms) {
  const from = cam.up.clone().normalize();
  const view = controls.target.clone().sub(cam.position).normalize();
